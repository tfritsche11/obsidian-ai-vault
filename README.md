# Obsidian AI Vault

A human-first, AI-friendly Obsidian vault.

## Principles

- Markdown is the source of truth.
- Human-authored notes are canonical.
- AI assists but does not overwrite canonical notes.
- Templates follow OKF v0.2 document conventions where applicable.
- AI-generated artifacts belong in `90 AI/`.

## Folder Overview

- 00 Inbox — capture
- 10 Areas — ongoing responsibilities
- 20 Projects — temporary work
- 30 Resources — reference material
- 40 Knowledge — evergreen notes
- 50 Journal — daily and periodic notes
- 60 Assets — images, PDFs, attachments
- 70 Templates — note templates
- 90 AI — generated summaries and reports
- 99 Archive — inactive material
